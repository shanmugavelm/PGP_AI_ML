Post Graduate Program In Artificial Intelligence And Machine Learning

Each entry below is a completed course project, in the order it was built — problem, approach, and the result it produced.

01

Python Foundations

FoodHub Order Data Analysis

A food aggregator wants to analyze its registered customers' order data to understand demand across restaurants and improve customer experience. As the hired Data Scientist, your task is to answer the team's key business questions through data analysis to support these improvements.

RESULT

The top 5 of 178 restaurants drive ~35% of order value, with American, Japanese, Italian, and Chinese as the most preferred cuisines (avg. cost ≤$15) and Vietnamese the least. Median order cost is ~$14, and weekend deliveries are notably faster.

02

Machine Learning

AllLife Bank — Personal Loan Campaign

The goal is to build a classification model that identifies key demographic and behavioral drivers of personal loan adoption among existing liability customers. These insights will enable targeted marketing, improving campaign conversion rates, optimizing spend, and driving profitable loan portfolio growth.

RESULT

Income (>$92.5k) and credit card spending (CCAvg >$2.95k) are the strongest predictors of loan acceptance, and should anchor customer targeting, refined further by education and family size for personalized messaging. With ~67% model precision, use score-based tiering instead of blanket campaigns to optimize spend, and prioritize deposit-only, high-income customers for upsell offers.

03

Advanced Machine Learning

EasyVisa — Visa Approval Prediction

In FY2016, OFLC processed 775,979 applications (up 9% YoY), making manual review increasingly unsustainable as volumes grow. EasyVisa has been hired to build a classification model that predicts visa approval likelihood and identifies key drivers of case status, helping streamline decisions on applicant certification or denial.

RESULT

Prioritize applicants with strong job experience, appropriate education levels, and annual (rather than hourly) wage offers, as these strongly predict approval. Focus screening on high-approval regional and demographic profiles (e.g., Asian applicants in key US regions) while dropping low-impact features to streamline the model. Deploy the model to automate initial screening and reduce OFLC's manual review workload.
